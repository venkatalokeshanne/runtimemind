# Behavioral Interview Prep

Tell concise, evidence-backed stories.

## Structure
- Situation
- Task
- Action
- Result

## Tips
- Lead with outcomes and metrics
- Be specific about your contribution
- Show learning loops and trade-offs
- Keep answers 2–3 minutes

## Quick prompts
- Conflict and resolution
- Leading through ambiguity
- Handling production incidents
- Improving team velocity
